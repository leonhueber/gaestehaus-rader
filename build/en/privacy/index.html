2. How we process personal data

Protecting your personal data matters to us. We process personal data only in accordance with applicable law, in particular the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

This policy explains what personal data we process when you use our website or Svelte app, to what extent and for what purposes.

3. Web analytics with Plausible (without cookies)

We use Plausible Analytics to understand how people use our website and to improve it. Plausible uses no cookies and does not create personal user profiles.

The data processed may include:

  • Page or URL visited
  • Referring URL
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Date and time of access

Plausible processes IP addresses only in truncated (anonymised) form.

Purpose: measuring our audience and improving our website.
Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests).

Provider: Plausible Insights OÜ (Plausible Analytics). Data is sent to the Plausible endpoint at https://plausible.io/api/event.

4. Google Search Console

We use Google Search Console to obtain technical information about how our website appears in Google Search, including crawling and indexing data.

Google Search Console does not process the personal data of visitors to our website. It sets no cookies, requires no opt-in and embeds no tracking scripts on our site.

Purpose: technical analysis and improvement of our visibility in search engines.
Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests).

5. Hosting by DomainFactory

Our website is hosted by:

DomainFactory GmbH

When you visit our website, DomainFactory processes the technical data needed to operate the site securely and reliably (server logs).

The following data may be processed:

  • Visitor’s IP address
  • Date and time of access
  • Page or URL visited
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URL

Purpose: secure operation and troubleshooting.
Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests).

DomainFactory processes these data on our behalf as a processor under Article 28 GDPR.

Retention depends on DomainFactory’s server log settings and is usually a few days.

6. Contact by email or phone

If you contact us by email or phone, we process the information you provide, such as your name, contact details and the content of your enquiry, so we can respond.

Purpose: handling enquiries.
Legal basis:

  • Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into a contract) if your enquiry concerns a booking or service.
  • Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in communicating with you).

We delete your enquiry once it has been fully dealt with, unless statutory retention requirements apply.

7. Locally hosted fonts

Our website uses the Montserrat typeface. We store the font on our own server and load it from there.

Loading this font does not connect your browser to Google or any other external provider. No personal data is transferred to third parties as a result of using the font.

Purpose: a consistent and appealing presentation of our website.
Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests).

8. Google Maps

“Get directions” and “Open directions in Google Maps” open the external service when clicked. The area map passes the guesthouse and selected destination as route points, not your device location.

Google: Privacy policy · Google Maps: Terms of use

8a. OpenStreetMap & FOSSGIS

The homepage area map uses map tiles supplied by the OpenStreetMap Foundation (OSMF) to show the village and region. It only requests tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org after you select “Load map”.

These requests disclose your IP address, browser/device information, referring page, requested map area and request time to OSMF or its delivery servers. OSMF uses a worldwide network of cache servers, so processing outside the EU is possible. Its privacy policy explains storage and further processing.

We do not request your device location or persist map activation. “Close map” stops further map requests; it does not undo data already transmitted.

Routes were prepared using routing.openstreetmap.de, operated by FOSSGIS e. V. (OSRM), and are delivered with our website. Selecting a destination or travel mode does not send a request to that routing service. Walking and driving times are estimates without live traffic, closures, weather or parking and do not replace current navigation.

Legal basis for voluntarily loading external maps: Article 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent). You can stop further embedding at any time by selecting “Close map”.

OpenStreetMap Foundation: Privacy policy · Routing service information

You can book apartments online through our website. The Nassfeld-Pressegger See tourism region provides the external booking platform, which is embedded in our website via an iframe. Opening the booking form connects your browser directly to the respective providers’ servers.

  • IP address
  • Date and time of access
  • Browser and device information
  • Booking and contact details you enter in the form
  • Details of the accommodation and travel dates you select, where applicable

The respective providers are independently responsible for processing booking data. We have no influence over the nature, extent or purpose of data processing within the booking form.

Purpose: providing online booking.
Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract or steps prior to entering into a contract).

Depending on the services used by the providers, personal data may be transferred to third countries, for example outside the EU.

For more information, see the providers’ privacy policies:

  • Nassfeld-Pressegger See Tourismus GmbH: Privacy policy
  • feratel: Privacy policy

10. Disclosure of personal data

We share personal data with third parties only if this is necessary to perform a contract, we are legally required to do so, or you have given your consent.

Plausible Insights OÜ may receive data when Plausible Analytics is used (section 3). Google Maps opens via an external link (section 8). When you use online booking, Nassfeld-Pressegger See Tourismus GmbH and feratel may receive data (section 9). If you activate the local area map, the OpenStreetMap Foundation also receives the access data described in section 8a.

To exercise your rights, please contact us at: info@rader-gitschtal.at
