Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the most important questions about the stay and amenities.

How many holiday apartments are there?

Guesthouse Rader offers three holiday apartments.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is available from 2:00 pm and check-out is by 10:00 am.

Are Wi-Fi and parking available?

Yes. Free high-speed Wi-Fi is available in all apartments, and free parking is directly in front of the guesthouse.

Are pets allowed?

No. Pets are not allowed at Guesthouse Rader.

Where is Guesthouse Rader located?

The guesthouse is located in Weißbriach in the Gitschtal valley in Carinthia and serves as a quiet base for trips to Lake Weissensee and Nassfeld.

How can I book or get in touch?

Booking requests go through the booking page, while direct contact is available by phone or email.
